A BRIGHTER CELEBRATION STARTS SAFELY

Enjoy the moment.
Protect each other.

01

Choose a safe place

Use fireworks only where legally permitted, outdoors, and away from buildings, vehicles and flammable materials.

02

Follow the label

Read and follow every instruction. Never dismantle, modify or relight a malfunctioning firework.

03

Keep your distance

Keep children supervised by adults and spectators at a safe distance. Never hold a firework unless its label specifically permits it.

04

Be considerate

Follow local noise, timing and environmental rules. Consider pets, older people and neighbours.

Buy and use only legally compliant products from authorised sellers. Follow all applicable local laws and official safety guidance.
